You are a police officer called for a crime scene in Montflorit. The crime scene is a big, wealthy house. When you arrive, you notice that there is high fence at the entrance and the front door is visible from the street. The front door was not forced.
Inside, your colleagues inform you that there was a burglary and a murder. The victim is Montse, the grandma living in the property. Her body was found in the kitchen floor. She was lying face-up, with a knife stuck in her back. The forensic team is working on the crime scene and the results from laboratory analysis will be available tomorrow.
On the other hand, your colleagues inform you that the burglars stole 250 000 euros in cash and more than 700 000 euros worth of jewellery and watches.
You must interrogate the witnesses. Remember you can only ask questions; you cannot make assumptions or lie to your witnesses. You can choose the order of interrogation, and you can interrogate the same witness twice. You can also call new witnesses if you feel this is necessary. You can take notes.
